**Zicong Fan (Alex)**
I am a scientist at Meshcapade working on learning hands in action with expertise in 3D hand pose estimation, 3D human/hand-object reconstruction and generative synthesis. My PhD is at ETH Zürich supervised by Max Planck Institute Director [Michael J. Black](https://ps.is.mpg.de/~black), ETH Zürich Professors [Otmar Hilliges](https://ait.ethz.ch/people/hilliges/) and [Marc Pollefeys](https://people.inf.ethz.ch/marc.pollefeys/). I co-organize the HANDS workshop series at ICCV/ECCV (2023–2025), a leading venue for hand perception and interaction in computer vision. My work integrates computer vision, deep learning, and 3D reasoning to build robust models for human-centric understanding. Before starting my Ph.D., I finished my B.Sc. (2018) and M.Sc. (2020) in Computer Science at The University of British Columbia, Vancouver, Canada. In my bachelor, I worked with Professor [Ian M. Mitchell](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~mitchell/) in robotics. In my Masters, I worked in vision + language problems with Professor [Leonid Sigal](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~lsigal/) and Professor [Jim Little](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~little/).
